Title: Travel and Expense Policy
Employees must submit expense reports within 14 days of incurring a cost.
Airfare should be booked in economy class unless the flight exceeds six hours.
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 75 dollars per day.
All expenses over 200 dollars require a scanned receipt attached to the report.
Personal expenses mixed with business expenses will not be reimbursed.
